# Investment research workflow

1. Enter a ticker in the company workspace. Inspect source dates and coverage, then edit highlighted forecast assumptions. The live result, market/analyst comparison, driver analysis and reverse DCF use the existing valuation engines.
2. For relative valuation, inspect the four suggested candidates and explicitly include suitable peers. Latest annual ratios are not trailing-twelve-month data. Choose the disclosed CUIG constant-multiple scenario or supply genuinely forward, source-dated peer estimates for matched-forward mode. Inspect each peer contribution, exclusions and outlier flags.
3. Expand Investment thesis and saved research. Give the research a title and scenario name, then record the thesis, catalysts, risks, assumptions, questions and evidence. Written notes are your interpretation; source metadata remains with the financial snapshot.
4. Save research revision. The server recomputes the model from the exact submitted financials and assumptions and creates a private immutable revision. The saved context is frozen. Loading another company cannot overwrite those financials.
5. To save a changed valuation scenario, first edit assumptions in the company workspace, then choose Use active valuation in the research panel. Save with a new scenario name. To edit only written notes, open the research report and choose Edit thesis and save a revision; no provider refresh is required.
6. Open the research library to revisit revisions. Older research is pageable. Reports show the newest 50 scenarios sharing the exact financial-input hash; refreshed company/price inputs form a separate group. No case probabilities are assigned.
7. Download complete research JSON or print/save the report as PDF. Formula-driven model Excel exports the frozen valuation inputs, results and formulas. Written thesis notes and scenario tables are included in JSON/print, not the model workbook. Exports do not fetch a new price.

Research is private to its signed browser library or account. Optional sign-in claims research saved in that browser and makes it available to other signed-in sessions. Keep the browser cookie if working anonymously. Accounts currently have no verified-email or email recovery service; do not expect a reset email.

DCF, DDM and relative research snapshots are supported. Residual-income calculations remain available in the existing valuation suite, but their thesis persistence is not included in this release. Source coverage, company suitability and independent filing verification remain separate from mathematical agreement. No generated financial facts or AI-written source claims are inserted into notes.

API: `GET /api/research` returns up to 50 owned summaries; `?ticker=AAPL` filters and `?before=OWNED_ID` retrieves older summaries. `POST /api/research` accepts title, scenario_name, method, financials, assumptions, thesis and an optional owned parent_id for the same company/method. `GET /api/research/ID`, `/research/ID`, and `/research/ID/export/json|xlsx` require ownership. Exports and private pages are no-store; workbook downloads use the existing reference request limit. No public research-sharing endpoint is enabled.
