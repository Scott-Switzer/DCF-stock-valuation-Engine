# Growth, peer and price decision references

Company loads remain independent of analyst availability. The browser fetches optional analyst references through `GET /api/references/<ticker>` after the core form is ready. Public Yahoo analysis-page JSON supplies annual `revenueEstimate.growth` (never EPS growth), average/range/coverage, dated fiscal periods, reported current market cap and analyst mean/median/range price targets. The provider can change or omit its page format; unavailable references are displayed explicitly without preventing valuation.

Normalized references are cached for one hour, with the original retrieval timestamp; failures are cached for five minutes. HTML is not persisted. Analyst modules are collected across issuer-bound packets, with a verified USD price identity. Reference GETs have a separate 20-per-client-per-minute atomic limit across tickers; calculation POST limits remain separate. The bounded upstream budget is eight seconds. Peer current-market-cap requests run concurrently after target references arrive. Loading references neither calculates nor saves a valuation.

Historical revenue growth and CAGR use reported annual revenue. Dividend growth uses total common cash dividends paid to match the DDM forecast basis; a separately labeled per-share proxy divides each fiscal year's common dividends by that year's diluted weighted-average shares. Missing/zero baselines yield unavailable growth. Neither history nor consensus silently changes assumptions. Fiscal-year consensus can refer to a completed year awaiting results; do not assume provider “current year” means forecast year one.

Comparable suggestions rank curated industry/product/segment overlap and a live price × annual diluted-shares market-equity size proxy. The cards disclose size mismatches, mixed-business caveats and unavailable fit. Separately fetched reported current market caps appear when available. This taxonomy is an explainable starting selection; it does not verify segment revenue weights, competitive equivalence, accounting uniformity or claim to be a full peer-screening database. Known candidates without industry or product overlap are excluded before network loading (for example HPE is excluded from Apple’s computer competitors). Amazon mixes retail and cloud candidates. GOOG/GOOGL are not presented as separate peers for the same issuer.

Loaded financial fields and peer observations are read-only until the user explicitly unlocks overrides. Forecast assumptions remain directly editable; read-only fields still submit normally. Provider identity/provenance and override validation remain on the server. Field tooltips and the source-document disclosure identify sources and dates. The repeated collection banner is removed; the privacy footer and page remain available.

Result pages compare the model's 12-month scenario with the loaded dated market price and analyst consensus 12-month target reference. Present intrinsic value remains separately visible. Analyst target revision dates are unavailable; retrieval date is shown, with the provider's horizon limitation. These price comparisons exclude dividends and are not total-return forecasts. Analyst references are optional current observations shown at render time, separate from the saved valuation snapshot and its assumptions.
