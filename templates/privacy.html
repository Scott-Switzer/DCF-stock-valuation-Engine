Privacy & saved valuations

On the public Cloudflare deployment, clicking Save valuation (or submitting through a legacy Calculate form or calculation API) saves the submitted financial inputs, forecast assumptions, sources, result, ticker, valuation method, model version and submission time to a private Cloudflare D1 database. Live previews and exports do not create records. Calculations made with the local Python app are not collected.

Accounts are optional. Creating one stores an email address, which is also your sign-in name, in the private database. No verification email is sent yet, so the address is not verified. Passwords are stored only as salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes. A signed-in session uses an HttpOnly, Secure cookie; the database keeps only a digest of the session token, and sessions expire after 14 days. Valuations, research revisions, templates, watchlist entries and shares saved while signed in are linked to an internal account identifier rather than your email. Signing in moves the library saved in this browser into the account.

Without an account, a signed browser cookie keeps your saved library in this browser. A daily salted hash of your network address supports abuse prevention and duplicate detection. The raw address is not saved in the valuation database. Cloudflare may process network metadata to provide hosting and security.

Saving research also stores your title, named scenario, thesis notes, evidence notes and an immutable dated copy of the financial inputs, assumptions and recomputed model result. Research pages and exports require the same browser library or signed-in account that owns the record. Editing notes creates another revision; existing snapshots remain unchanged. Print and JSON include your notes; model Excel includes the valuation inputs and formulas.

Individual submissions are not publicly accessible. The site owner may analyze them for research or publish grouped summaries. Synthetic examples are flagged and excluded from sentiment analysis; identical submissions from the same daily network hash are deduplicated. These are self-selected assumptions, not a representative measure of market sentiment or investment advice.

Do not submit confidential financials or personal information. Records are retained for research; request deletion by emailing scswitzer@chapman.edu with your saved reference or the email on your account. Deletion is handled by the owner. No advertising or sale of submissions is enabled.
